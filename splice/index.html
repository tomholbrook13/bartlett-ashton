SPLICE

No. 1
How to play
  1. Each strip is two words woven together. Every word keeps its letters in order.
  2. Tap a letter to put it in the pink word, again for the blue word, and again to clear it.
  3. When every letter is used and both strands spell words, the strip is solved.
  4. Three strips a day, from 4 + 4 letters up to 6 + 6. Each has only one answer.
  5. Stuck? Hint places one correct letter in the first unsolved strip.
