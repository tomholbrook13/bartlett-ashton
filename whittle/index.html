WHITTLE

No. 1
How to play
  1. Today's word is hidden in a bag of eight letters.
  2. Start with any three-letter word from the bag.
  3. Grow it: keep every letter, add one more from the bag, and rearrange them into a new word.
  4. Reach eight letters and you've found today's word. A word that can't grow into it won't be accepted.
  5. Stuck? Hint shows which letter to add. Tap it again to see a word.
