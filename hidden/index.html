HIDDEN

No. 1
How to play
  1. The paragraph hides several words on today's theme.
  2. Every hidden word runs across a gap between words. "Slow learner" hides OWL. Ignore spaces and punctuation.
  3. Tap a word to see it and its neighbours up close. Then drag across the big letters, or tap the first letter and then the last.
  4. The boxes below show how many words there are and how long each one is.
  5. Stuck? Hint shows where a word starts. Tap it again to reveal that word.
