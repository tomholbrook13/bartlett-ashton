CROSSLINE

No. 1
How to play
  1. Two cities are joined by the shortest route over the globe, the way a plane would fly.
  2. Find every country the line passes through on the way. The cities' own countries are given.
  3. Tap a country on the map, or type its name. Small countries are easier to type.
  4. Wrong guesses count as misses. Stuck? Hint reveals the next country along the route.
