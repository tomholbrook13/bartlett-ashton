How to play
  1. Five words are hidden in the rings. Turn the rows to spell one of them down any column, one letter from each row.
  2. When you find one, its column locks and the leftover letters spin onto smaller rings.
  3. Keep going until all five words are locked. The last one drops into place by itself.
  4. Tap the arrows, drag a row, or use ↑↓ to pick a row and ←→ to turn it.
  5. Every click is one turn. Stuck? Set a row turns one row to where the nearest word needs it.
