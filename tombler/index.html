How to play
  1. Five words are written top to bottom in the columns. Each row of letters has been spun out of place.
  2. Turn the rows left or right until every column spells a word. Rows wrap around.
  3. Tap the arrows, drag a row, or use ↑↓ to pick a row and ←→ to turn it.
  4. Every click of a row is one turn. Try to open the lock in par. Stuck? Set a row snaps one into place.
